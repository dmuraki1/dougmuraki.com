About

I own Sunfish Pool Service and I run it full time. Before this I spent twenty years in service work, and I'd wanted to build something of my own for a long time.

I like work where you serve people directly. A pool company lets me do that every day: real customers I can meet, real problems I can fix, and a crew I get to build up myself.

I run the company, not the routes. Our techs handle the pools, and my job is to make sure they have the training, the equipment and the support to show up the same day every week and keep the water right. When something goes wrong, I'm the one you'll hear from.

The crew

I believe in treating the people who work here right. I hire, train and support every tech, and I put real time into teaching them, because the company is only as good as the person standing at your pool.

I'm building Sunfish so the people on my crew can grow into running it. Every tech who joins isn't just an employee. They could be the one leading this company someday.

I want to build them up to take over my role someday.
