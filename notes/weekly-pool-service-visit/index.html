What a weekly pool service visit actually covers

Doug Muraki, owner of Sunfish Pool Service, Sacramento
October 28, 2026

Here's what a Sunfish tech actually does at your pool every week.

You get a text when they're on the way. They test the water for chlorine, pH, alkalinity and calcium and balance it, then skim the surface, brush the walls and steps, empty the skimmer and pump baskets, and check the equipment while it's running.

When the work's done you get a photo of the pool and a water report sent to your phone. Same day every week, no contract.

That's the whole visit. Most of what makes it work isn't the checklist, it's doing it the same way every time so you stop thinking about the pool.

If you're in Sacramento, Carmichael, Citrus Heights, Antelope or Fair Oaks and want someone to take that over, we'll get you taken care of.
